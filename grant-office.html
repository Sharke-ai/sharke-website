Questions

Before you start.

How is this different from the grant team?

The grant team prepares the first draft; you review and submit. The grant office prepares the full application package, submits with your approval and includes the Funding Goal Guarantee. Both build and maintain your pipeline. Compare the plans.

What work will we still need to do?

Share your core program and budget materials, confirm your organization's program and impact details, and approve each application. Your team also handles funder conversations and any final portal step that requires your own account.

What if we already have a grant writer?

Keep them involved. Sharke can handle the research, fit checks and pipeline work, while your grant writer contributes organizational knowledge and reviews the application.

Do we need grants in mind before starting?

No. Sharke starts from your organization's profile and funding goal, then identifies opportunities and funder relationships to evaluate.

Who handles our information?

Sharke uses your materials to provide the service, with service providers that support processing, storage and delivery. See our Privacy Policy for how information is handled.

Can we cancel and keep our work?

Yes. The plan is month to month. Reply to a Sharke email to cancel at the end of the current billing period. The pipeline, verdicts and drafts remain yours.
