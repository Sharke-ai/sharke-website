Where less support was visible: the youth-transition organization had three matched foundation funders and the rare-disease research organization had eight, compared with 16 for food education and 24 for media. Education varied widely: one literacy organization had two, while the other three education organizations had 23 to 47.

What nonprofits can learn: where fewer foundation relationships were visible for organizations in this group, and where missing records need checking. It gives context for funding conversations without setting anyone's priorities.

This counts funders with matched award records, not available grants, dollars needed or pipeline strength. The arts and human-services cases remain incomplete, not evidence of no funding. This small, selected group does not establish that a whole priority area is underfunded.

How to read this example

Education groups B, C, H and I. Their matched-funder counts are 38, 47, 2 and 23 respectively. Every other area represents one organization. These are the same ten selected organizations in the existing cohort sample, not a representative survey or a Sharke customer cohort.

We matched recipient tax IDs to a local extract of itemized IRS Form 990-PF awards dated 2021 to 2023, removed repeated record IDs and counted distinct foundation funders. Funder identity was checked by tax ID. Repeated awards from one foundation count as one funder. The count measures the breadth of recorded support, not its size, adequacy or current availability.

Program-area labels use published organization descriptions and the original sample classifications; they are descriptive groupings, not grant-purpose classifications or a claim that each award funded that program. Organization A retains its broad recorded human-services category because its program area was not confirmed. Public descriptions were checked October 23, 2026; the award records remain historical.

Missing records stay missing. A has two possible name-only award records without a recipient tax ID; the match remains unresolved. E has no tax-ID match in the extract. Neither is counted as an unfunded organization. Name variants, incomplete filings and unmatched awards can hide support.

Community-foundation, donor-advised-fund, public-charity, government funding, direct corporate giving and individual giving are outside this private-foundation view. Organization size, age, geography and funding need differ and have not been normalized. The sample cannot establish which fields have less funding available or weaker pipelines.

Source lineage: the existing anonymized cohort sample and its archived organization mapping; recipient-matched Form 990-PF award extract for 2021 to 2023. An internal review record retains source hashes, matching logic and row-level evidence. These are public-record observations, not independently audited customer outcomes.
