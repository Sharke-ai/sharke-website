Questions

Before you decide

How is this different from a grant-matching database?

A matching database hands you a longer list to triage. Sharke checks the list against documented funder behavior and tells you what deserves staff time.

Do we have to sign a contract?

No. Subscriptions are month to month, and you can cancel anytime. The Assessment is a one-time purchase.

Self-serve or done-for-you: which one is us?

It comes down to who owns grants. If someone on your team can do the work while Sharke does the finding, verifying, and drafting, that is self-serve, $159 a month. If no one on your team owns grants and you need the whole function handled, that is done for you, from $249 a month. Not sure you have a funding gap at all? Run the free Checkup first; it shows you in about five minutes.
