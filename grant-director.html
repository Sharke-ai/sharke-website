Questions

Before you decide

When can we start?

Today. Sign up and your workspace is live the same day: Sharke starts finding, verifying, and prioritizing your pipeline from the first login, with drafting support and deadline tracking included.

We already track grants in a spreadsheet. Is this one more system to learn?

No. The pipeline, the verdicts, and the drafts live in one place that replaces the spreadsheet, not another tool that feeds it. And if you run a CRM, keep it: Sharke is not a CRM and is not asking you to move anything. Most teams are reviewing their first verified grants the same day they sign up.

What happens when our development person moves on?

That is exactly what Sharke protects against. The average development professional stays around 16 to 18 months, and the median vacancy runs six months (CompassPoint and the Haas, Jr. Fund, UnderDeveloped). A pipeline that lives in one person's head leaves with them. One that lives in Sharke is inherited by the next person on day one: verdicts, funders, deadlines and all.

Is this AI, or is a person involved?

Both. Sharke does the research and drafting, and every verdict is built from documented funder filings, 990s, award history, and eligibility, not AI guesses. And a person stands behind it: the founder signs off on every verdict.

Are there even grants for an organization our size?

Yes. Many funders give specifically to organizations in your range, and some prefer first-time and smaller grantees. Sharke reads each funder's giving history and shows you the ones whose record fits your size, so you are not chasing funders who only write large checks to large organizations.

Why not just use the free funder directories?

They are real and they are good at what they do, which is hand you names. If names are what you are short of, use them. What none of them do is tell you which of those names has ever funded an organization at your size, in your state, doing your work. That is where the hours go, and that is the part Sharke does.

Who sees the financials and materials we upload?

Only Sharke, and only to find, verify, and draft grants for your organization. Your materials are never shared or sold, and they are not used to train anything. Your organization owns its data.

If we cancel, do we keep our pipeline?

It is month to month, and the work is yours. You can export your verdicts, funders, and deadlines at any time, so what you build in Sharke stays with your organization whether you continue or not. What we would rather you notice is that the answer moves: funders file, priorities shift, and a Move on from last quarter is not always a Move on now.

Do we have to sign a contract?

No. It is month to month, and you can cancel anytime.
