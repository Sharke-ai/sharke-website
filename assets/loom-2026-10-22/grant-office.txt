A grant deadline is coming up, but your team already has a full workload. Here is how Sharke helps you check a funder before committing that time.

First, we work out how much those programs need and subtract the funding already committed. We don't count pending applications as money you have.

Then we build a plan to close that gap: grants worth applying for and funders worth getting to know. That plan is your funding pipeline.

We check who those funders have supported before. For example, Dollar General's published award lists include two literacy organizations in Durham: Augustine Literacy Project Triangle and Students to Scholars. That history makes its Youth Literacy Grants worth investigating for Riverbend's next funding cycle. We still need to confirm Riverbend meets the requirements.

Next, we prepare the application materials. This draft explains Riverbend's tutoring program and flags what we still need to confirm, including student numbers and the budget.
