Review funding options: a smaller share of nonprofits received foundation grants than the state benchmark, and foundation dollars per nonprofit were less than half that benchmark. This flags a potential funding risk to investigate, rather than a confirmed shortage.

Child care: a concern across all three states.

Georgia has two recorded funders for 38 nonprofits; North Carolina has eight for 53. Texas has more funders, but still meets both concern measures. This pattern suggests that finding more names may not be enough. Compare the available grants with the amount and type of support these programs need.

Georgia: the type of health work matters.

Health support services have five recorded funders for 29 nonprofits. Community clinics have 48 for 33. Treating both as simply health care would hide this difference. Health support organizations may need a different mix of funding sources.

For nonprofits

Check which funders are open to new applicants, what they support and how much funding they can offer. Use this to build a realistic funding plan and identify where you need additional sources.

For capacity builders

Look for funding gaps shared by nonprofits in your cohort. Where existing options cannot meet their needs, work with funders on new grant programs, pooled support or public funding.

How to read this comparison

The review covers nonprofits with annual revenue of $100,000 to $10 million. Funder counts and dollars use the most recent filing year in the analysis; grant reach uses its three-year review window. Each state benchmark covers education, health and human services nonprofits in that revenue band. Figures come from matched private-foundation grant records, not a list of currently open opportunities. A funder can appear in more than one cell. No flag does not establish that funding is sufficient.

Background sources: nonprofit public filings and sample Assessment targets. Foundation-history figures use grants reported in IRS Form 990-PF tax filings. Shares compare the foundation grants recorded for each organization.
