What your members receive

The grant work keeps moving throughout the year.

Every organization starts with a Grant Funding Viability Assessment: where its funding stands, its runway, the pipeline its goal would take and funders worth verifying.

Sharke then builds and maintains the pipeline with the organization, checking grant and funder fit, tracking deadlines and identifying the next opportunities to pursue. Each organization contributes its program knowledge and makes its funding decisions.

This can sit alongside the training and capacity-building programs you already support.

What your association receives

Satisfaction tells your board the organizations liked it. A before-and-after tells them what changed.

At the start and at months three, six, nine and twelve, you see the pipeline each organization's funding goal requires and how much of it is in place. Each is measured the same way.

  • The quarterly report: progress against each organization's pipeline target, plus its largest funder and that funder's renewal history, refreshed as filings arrive.
  • The annual edition: foundation funding and need in your area, where foundation money is thin, and what the organizations reported in their filings. Each section carries its own as-of date.
What does the annual edition include?

It covers new grantees of the foundations beside you, concentration of foundation support among organizations of your members' size, where foundation money is thin, local need, and the work organizations reported. Reported activities are not presented as independently measured outcomes.

Foundations beside you are those that funded two or more of your members in the two years before. A new grantee is one a foundation had not funded in those two years. Filing dates are shown because public records lag current activity.
