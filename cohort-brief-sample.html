Sample cohort brief

What one cohort of 14 nonprofits looked like at the start.

This is the brief a capacity builder receives: every organization in the cohort on one page, measured the same way, so you can show your board and funders where each one started and, a year later, what changed.

Sample. We built this for 14 real nonprofits funded by one community foundation, found in that foundation's public grant records, using public records only. We removed the names of the foundation and the organizations. Every number is unchanged. In a real cohort, you choose the organizations.

Nearly a quarter of the funders on these organizations' combined list could not make them a grant at all. At 20 to 30 hours per application, that is 460 to 690 hours for every 100 applications built from an unscreened list.

The 20 to 30 hours is our planning figure for one application, not a measurement inside this cohort.

4. The starting line

This is the baseline. There is no earlier cycle to compare against, and the brief does not invent one. At the end of the year we run it again for the same organizations, and the change for each one is what you report.
