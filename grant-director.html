Questions

Before you start

What work will I still need to do?

Confirm your organization's profile, provide the materials for drafting, and decide which opportunities to pursue. Sharke handles research, fit checks, deadline tracking and the first draft. You confirm your organization's program and impact details, review the draft and submit.

What happens after I sign up?

Your workspace is available the same day. Confirm your mission, location and budget, then review your pipeline and the evidence behind the recommendations. Add your organization's materials when you are ready to prepare an application draft.

How is the monthly price set?

Organizations with annual revenue under $1M pay $99 a month; $1M to $3M pay $159; over $3M pay $249. Choose the band shown on your latest 990. We confirm it from the public filing at signup. If it differs, we show you the evidence and the choice stays yours.

We already use a spreadsheet or CRM. Do we have to move everything?

Keep your CRM. Sharke holds the grant opportunities, evidence, deadlines and drafts for this work. You can export your pipeline information to share with your team or board.

Is a person involved?

Yes. Sharke uses AI for research and drafting, with recommendations checked against documented funder evidence. Recommendations are reviewed before they reach your team, with the evidence and any uncertainty made clear. You retain the final decision on what to pursue and submit.

Who sees the materials we upload?

Sharke uses your materials to research, verify and draft grants for your organization. Your materials are never shared or sold, and they are not used to train AI models. Your organization owns its data.

Can we cancel and keep our work?

Yes. The plan is month to month and you can cancel anytime. Export your verdicts, funders and deadlines before you leave, so the pipeline you build stays with your organization.
