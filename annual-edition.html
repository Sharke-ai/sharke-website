The annual edition: one report on the organizations you support, for your board's planning meeting.

It starts from each organization's own filings and public survey data, so the organizations write nothing. $2,500, once a year, delivered within 5 business days of your intake.

What is in it

  • What the organizations reportedThe people they reported serving and what they spent on programs, from their latest two public filings, in their own words.
  • Need in your areaHow many people need the help these organizations give, and where, from the American Community Survey, set against the foundation dollars that reach the nonprofits doing that work.
  • Where foundation money is thinIn your focus area and region, the kinds of work and the places where nonprofits of their size get the least visible foundation support.
  • New grantees of the foundations beside youHow many grants they made in the latest filed year to new grantees in your state, nonprofits with budgets of $100,000 to $10 million, with the total and the typical grant. A new grantee is one the foundation had not funded in the two years before. Foundations beside you are the ones that funded two or more of these organizations in the two years before.
  • The need in your stateHow many nonprofits of their size get more than half of their foundation support from one foundation, from public filings.

Every section names its sources and limits and carries its own date, because filings run about two years behind.
