Grant planning

Check grant fit before committing application time.

A grant can sound right for your mission and still have an eligibility, geography or budget constraint that changes the decision. Check those details before committing your team.

Build the decision from evidence.

  • Eligibility: read the current program requirements, including geography, applicant type and any fiscal-sponsorship rules.
  • Funder history: look at the work and organizations the funder has supported. Past awards are evidence to consider, not a guarantee or an automatic eligibility rule.
  • Timing: confirm the current deadline and whether an invitation or conversation is needed before applying.
  • Your capacity: consider the application work, required partnerships and delivery obligations alongside your other opportunities.

Keep uncertainty visible.

If a requirement or funding pattern cannot be confirmed, record the question and resolve it before relying on the opportunity. A convincing description is not a substitute for current program requirements.

A pipeline brings those decisions together: what to pursue now, what needs cultivation and what needs more evidence. That gives drafting work a clear purpose.
