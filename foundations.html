What does the annual edition include?

It covers new grantees of the foundations beside you, concentration of foundation support among organizations of your grantees' size, where foundation money is thin, local need, the work organizations reported, and organizations to explore beyond the supplied list. Reported activities are not presented as independently measured outcomes.

Foundations beside you are those that funded two or more of your grantees in the two years before. A new grantee is one a foundation had not funded in those two years. Filing dates are shown because public records lag current activity.

What your grantees receive

The grant work keeps moving throughout the year.

Every organization starts with a Grant Funding Viability Assessment: where its funding stands, its runway, the pipeline its goal would take and funders worth verifying.

Sharke then builds and maintains the pipeline with the organization, checking grant and funder fit, tracking deadlines and identifying the next opportunities to pursue. Each organization contributes its program knowledge and makes its funding decisions.

Recommendations are reviewed before they reach your team, with the evidence and any uncertainty made clear.

This can sit alongside the training and capacity-building programs you already support.
