Sample cohort brief

What a cohort of 10 nonprofits would look like.

This is the brief you would receive: every organization in your cohort on one page, measured the same way, so you can show your board and funders where each one starts and, a year later, what changed.

Sample. The figures come from the public records of 10 real nonprofits, with their names removed. Your cohort's numbers will be its own.

In this sample, 23% of the funders on the cohort's combined list could not make these organizations a grant at all. At 20 to 30 hours per application, that is 460 to 690 hours for every 100 applications built from an unscreened list.

The 20 to 30 hours is our planning figure for one application, not a measurement of any cohort.

Funder market is how much comparable grant activity the public record shows for organizations like this one. Four of the 10 sit in a limited or thin market, where more applications is the wrong move and checking the few real funders is the right one.
