Before you start

What the Assessment can tell you.

Where does the evidence come from?

We compare your IRS filings with organizations of similar size and mission, then examine visible funder award history. Your report cites its sources and explains where the record is incomplete.

Does this verify that a funder is accepting applications now?

No. Public filings run behind current activity. The Assessment identifies funders worth verifying and sizes the funding plan. Current eligibility, application cycles and live fit are checked in the next stage of work.

What if the evidence does not support our plan?

The report explains the constraint and the next steps: for example, adjusting the timeline or considering a different mix of funding. You receive a clear call and its supporting evidence.

What if we have limited filing history?

The Assessment is strongest with three or more years of IRS filings. A thinner record is identified as a limitation; missing evidence is not filled in.

Is this a subscription?

No. The Assessment costs $79 once. If you later want Sharke to build and maintain the pipeline, the grant team and grant office are separate monthly services.
