Questions

Before you decide

Why can't my grant writer do this?

A strong grant writer can review fit. But assembling current award history, first-time grantee patterns, budget comparables, geography, timing, and relationship requirements on every opportunity is time-consuming. Sharke makes that verification a standard step before writing begins.

Is this another grant database?

No. Databases help you find opportunities. Sharke determines whether an opportunity belongs in the funding plan before staff time is committed.

Why not just use ChatGPT or Claude to find grants?

A chatbot reasons from a grant's description and whatever you paste in. It has no award records, so it cannot check who a funder actually funds, at what sizes, in which states, or how often a first-time applicant gets in. Sharke's verdicts are checked against more than 3.8 million grant award records and the funder's own filings, and when the record shows no real path, Sharke says Move on. A chatbot will happily keep you optimistic.

Why not use ChatGPT or Claude to write the application?

For prose, they are genuinely useful. The expensive mistake is not the writing, it is spending 20 to 30 hours writing for a grant that never had a real path. Sharke verifies the grant first, helps draft only what earns a place in the Verified Pipeline, and red-teams the application against the funder's criteria, priorities, and past awards before you submit. Your voice stays yours; the weak spots get caught before a funder sees them.

Can Sharke surface funders that never publish open calls?

Yes. Many foundations fund by invitation and relationship, not open RFPs. Sharke reads their actual giving from public filings, surfaces the ones already backing organizations like yours, and tells you which relationships are worth starting early. That is the grants-you-unlock stream in the diagram above: doors that open through relationships, not applications.

Do you guarantee funding?

No. Sharke never predicts awards or promises wins. Verification tells you whether the documented record supports pursuing an opportunity and where the path is weak, incomplete, or closed. On the pipeline plans there is one promise we do make, the Funding Goal Guarantee: If Sharke cannot identify enough verified opportunities and funder relationships to support your funding goal, we continue working at no charge until we do.

What do you need from us?

Your EIN and one line about your organization is all it takes. Sharke works from your public filings, peer grant records, and documented funder activity. Every decision identifies the evidence used and flags what cannot be verified.

What happens if the answer is no?

You still receive a decision path. The Assessment shows where the current plan is exposed, whether to narrow the path first, and which funders or assumptions to verify before more staff time is committed.
